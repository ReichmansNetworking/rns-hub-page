RNS Hub — Privacy

The RNS Hub is the private work system of Reichman's Networking Solutions LLC (Uhrichsville, Ohio). It is used by our owner, sales representatives and technicians to handle customer requests, quotes and installation jobs, and by customers to read and accept a quote we send them.

Sign-in with Google

Team members can sign in with their Google account. We receive only your name, email address and Google account ID, which we use to recognize you as a team member. We do not access your Gmail, contacts, files or anything else in your Google account, and we never post anything on your behalf.

What we keep

For customers: the contact details and messages you send us (for example through our website quote form or Facebook), the quotes we prepare, your acceptance of a quote, job notes, photos of the installation at your property and your sign-off. For team members: your name, email, role, sign-in events, and the job times you record.

How it is used and shared

Only to provide our services, run our business and keep required records. We do not sell or rent personal information. We use service providers to run the Hub (Supabase for the database and file storage, GitHub for hosting this page, Square for customer and payment records, and an email delivery service), each only for that purpose.

Security

Access is limited by role: sales representatives see only their own leads and customers, technicians see only the jobs assigned to them, and job photos are stored privately. Every change is recorded in a tamper-evident audit log.

Your choices

To ask what we hold about you, or to have it corrected or deleted where the law allows, contact us at rns.info.sales@gmail.com or (330) 390-4023.
